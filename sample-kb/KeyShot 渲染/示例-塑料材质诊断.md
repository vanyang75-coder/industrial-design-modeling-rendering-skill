# Sample - KeyShot Plastic Material Diagnosis

## Symptom

Plastic material looks flat, cheap, or too mirror-like.

## Next Actions

1. Confirm the model normals are correct and each part has the intended material.
2. Use one large soft highlight to reveal the product shape.
3. Adjust roughness after the lighting direction is readable.
4. Use subtle bump or texture only after the base highlight looks natural.

## Failure Checks

- If the product stays flat, rotate HDRI or add a rim light.
- If the plastic looks oily, increase roughness and reduce sharp reflections.
- If black faces appear, inspect normals and imported geometry.
